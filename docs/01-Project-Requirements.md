# Project Requirements

## Project Name

LegalDraft AI

---

## Problem Statement

Many individuals and small businesses require legal agreements such as Rental Agreements, Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), and Employment Agreements. Drafting these documents manually can be time-consuming and often requires legal expertise.

LegalDraft AI aims to simplify this process by generating professional first-draft legal agreements using Artificial Intelligence based on structured user input. The application assists users in preparing legal documents quickly while making it clear that the generated content should be reviewed before official legal use.

---

## Project Goal

Develop an AI-powered web application that enables users to:

- Select a legal agreement type
- Fill in required details through dynamic forms
- Generate a professional legal draft using Gemini AI
- Preview the generated agreement
- Interact with an AI Assistant to understand or improve the document
- Download the agreement as a PDF
- Store and manage previously generated documents

---

- Individuals

## Functional Requirements
